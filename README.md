# Digital Habits & Student Well-being Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the digital habits of 16,000 students, focusing on their daily Social Media and AI tool usage, to evaluate the direct impact on their physical and mental well-being. The primary objective is to extract actionable **human insights**, showcasing how digital behavior correlates with lifestyle metrics.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Language:** Python
* **Libraries:** Pandas (Data Cleaning & Manipulation), Matplotlib & Seaborn (Data Visualization)
* **Environment:** Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Key Business Insights
Through data segmentation and visualization, the analysis revealed two critical behavioral trends:

1. **The Screen-Time & Sleep Trade-off:** 
   There is a clear negative correlation between extended social media usage and sleep duration. 


2. **Mental Health Segmentation:**
   By segmenting the audience into *Light (0-3h)*, *Moderate (3-6h)*, and *Heavy (6+h)* users, a drop in the median Mental Health Score was identified among the Heavy User segment.
